## EQ comparison

_WBS INFO · Feb 28, 2008|41_

> well the difference is mainly it's functionality. Not every EQ has the same flexibility.
>
> M462 family sound the same as they are the same. The circuits are "ALMOST" identical. 
>
> M462 - 3 band full sweeps
> M462A - mid sweep and 2 fixed Q's on lf and hf
> M462B - 4 band full sweeps
>
> All the booster amps in the M462 series are the same, so they way it boosts the signal is identical.
> The M462 are balanced in nd balanced output so they could be patched in or put in line with the M470's insert send.
>
> Ward beck recycled circuits as most companies back then and still do. 
>
> The pre in the M490 is the SAME as the M470 and M480... the EQ in the M480 is derived from the M472
>
> The M472 is closest to the M462A, but with no mid sweep and a fixed high frequency. very simple. it is unbalanced and has no booster amp... the booster comes from the M470's EQ send and return. If you look closely at the M472 there are jumpers internally connected that short the IN and OUT where the M462's would plug in. Any "auxilary" that is balanced such as the M462, the M466 comps, M467 NG. M464 HP LP filters use the same in and out config. so on my console it's fitted all with M462's I could (if i wanted to) swap the EQ with a compressor... kinda cool!
>
> the M472 is simple and it shipped as the stock solution on most L472 consoles.
>
> I will say there are more EQ's like the M472A and the M472C that are stereo, then there are M522, M522A, M522B, M522C etc etc that are loosly based on the M462's.
>
> The EQ in the M460 is like the M462, infact they are the exact same cards. 
>
> In my opinion the M462A are the best, and having a bunch of M462B's have been really nice. 
>
> I will be selling a small rack of (6) M472's in the next week or so; my trip cost me more that I expected.
>
> Tones

> About fifteen years ago, I was doing occasional FOH sound for a youth theater company in the Montreal area. I was packing up our stock at the loading dock of the school we just played in, when the venue's tech showed me a box full of electronic stuff... he told me it was from a console Radio-Canada had given them. He said he didn't have much to do with that, and that I was welcome to take whatever I pleased. Since we were leaving, I didn't have much time to pick and choose, but I took a few "modules" (with the good-looking knobs!), transformers, faders and circuit cards, with the intention of scavenging the electronic components and use them in other electronic projects.
>
> I may have used a few components, but that stuff mainly laid dormant for many years. Only recently have I become aware that Ward-Beck stuff was getting desirable. Seeing pictures of WBS modules and cards, I realised that that loot was much more valuable than I thought... I had two M441 modules, one complete and the other dismantled. I reassembled the dismantled one according to the functional unit, made a case for the pair, and now I have two fine-sounding preamps that I regularly use to record. I also have a quad of M124 preamp cards that are waiting to get racked. Looking back, I should have taken more: I certainly would love to have a pair of M402 EQs...
